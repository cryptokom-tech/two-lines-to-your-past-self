## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Don't delete the voicemail your father left on 12 April, the one where he says he's proud of you.
> After his funeral you will replay it a hundred times, and it is the only recording of his voice you will ever have.

Line 1 warns the past self about one specific moment in a single sentence; line 2 says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. 213 characters, 2 lines, 1 sentence each — inside the 1–280 character gate.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
