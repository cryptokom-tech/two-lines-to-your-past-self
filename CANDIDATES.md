# Candidates — judged locally against the published judge brief

Judge weighs, most important first: (1) line 1 warns about one specific
moment, line 2 says why it mattered; (2) the moment is concrete enough to
picture (a time, a place, a decision, a sentence said out loud);
(3) line 2 shows real cause and effect, not a restatement;
(4) the two lines work as one, no wasted words;
(5) voice written to one specific person, plain and vivid, not clever
for its own sake. Scores are out of 5 per criterion, in that order.

## A — SUBMITTED (213 chars, 25/25)

> Don't delete the voicemail your father left on 12 April, the one where he says he's proud of you.
> After his funeral you will replay it a hundred times, and it is the only recording of his voice you will ever have.

5/5/5/5/5. Exact date plus a sentence said out loud; the loss is
irreversible and traced step by step (delete → no recording left →
replayed after the funeral); plain voice, to one person, no cleverness.

## B (213 chars, 23/25)

> At your grandmother's table on Christmas Eve, don't scroll through your phone while she tells the story about the war.
